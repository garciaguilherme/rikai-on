Credits & Acknowledgments

Rikai On is made possible thanks to these open tools, public APIs, browser standards, and community color palettes:

  • Anki & AnkiConnect (FooSoft) GitHub ↗

    Local Japanese vocabulary & spaced-repetition card state sync (leeches, hard, young, mature).

  • LRCLIB lrclib.net ↗

    Primary community-curated database of synchronized LRC song lyrics.

  • Kugou Music & NetEase Cloud Music

    Secondary Japanese LRC lyrics fallbacks when LRCLIB has no synchronized match.

  • YouTube (Player, oEmbed & Captions) youtube.com ↗

    Music playback, oEmbed track metadata, and Japanese player captions fallback.

  • Japan Top Charts (Apple / iTunes RSS, Billboard Japan, Wikipedia)

    Public Japanese Top Charts feeds used to update trending artists.

  • Web Intl.Segmenter API (ECMA-402)

    Native browser Japanese morphological word segmentation.

  • Community Color Palettes

    Catppuccin (Mocha & Latte), Dracula, Nord, Gruvbox, Tokyo Night, and Rosé Pine.
