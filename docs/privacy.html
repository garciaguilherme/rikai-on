Privacy Policy for Rikai On

Effective Date: November 22, 2026

Rikai On ("the Extension") is designed with privacy first. We do not operate any backend analytics, telemetry, or tracking servers, and we do not collect, sell, or share personal data.

1. Data Accessed and Stored Locally

  • Anki Vocabulary Data: When you trigger a sync, the Extension communicates strictly with your local AnkiConnect endpoint (127.0.0.1:8765 or localhost:8765) to categorize Japanese words into leech, hard, young, and mature lists. Your Anki data never leaves your computer and is cached solely in chrome.storage.local.
  • User Preferences & Personal Lists: Theme settings, interface language, manual known-word marks, favorite songs, discovered YouTube artists, custom artist lists, per-video subtitle sync offsets, and cached lyrics are stored exclusively in chrome.storage.local.

2. External Network Requests

  • Lyrics Providers (lrclib.net, kugou.com, music.163.com): Receives only the song/artist search query needed to retrieve synchronized Japanese LRC lyrics.
  • YouTube (youtube.com): On YouTube tabs, reads the video title, channel/author metadata (via public oEmbed), playback timestamp, and Japanese captions (only when external LRC lyrics are unavailable) strictly to render and synchronize lyrics locally on /watch pages. Public search results are also queried to resolve video IDs for discovered Japanese songs.
  • Japan Music Charts (itunes.apple.com, rss.marketingtools.apple.com, billboard-japan.com, ja.wikipedia.org): Fetches public Japanese chart artist names when updating the built-in artist discovery pool.

3. Data Sharing & Retention

The Extension never sells user data, displays third-party ads, or transmits user profiles to external servers. All locally stored preferences and caches can be cleared at any time by removing the Extension or clearing its storage in Chrome.

4. Contact

For support or privacy inquiries, please open an issue on the Rikai On GitHub repository or use the support contact listed on the Rikai On Chrome Web Store page.
